# Premium redesign plan

## Goal
Transform the current Beyond site into a more cinematic, editorial, and deliberately art-directed experience while preserving its authentic brand content.

## Implementation
- Strengthen the visual system with tighter typography, deeper tonal contrast, controlled champagne accents, fine rules, and restrained texture.
- Recompose the hero around the requested headline and a refined interactive metallic canvas treatment.
- Turn the statement, about, services, portfolio, and process into distinct editorial moments with purposeful scroll and hover behavior.
- Refine the testimonial, final call-to-action, full-screen mobile navigation, footer, and interaction states.
- Verify the experience at desktop and mobile sizes, including reduced motion, keyboard behavior, overflow, and runtime stability.

## Technical notes
- Keep the existing TanStack Start, React, Tailwind v4, optimized WebP assets, and lightweight canvas approach.
- Use transform and opacity animation paths, IntersectionObserver, and requestAnimationFrame only where necessary.
- Preserve semantic markup, focus visibility, dialog behavior, form validation, and reduced-motion fallbacks.
